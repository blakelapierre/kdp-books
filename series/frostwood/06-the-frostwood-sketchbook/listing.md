## Description (1,899 characters; KDP limit is 4,000)
```html
<b>Every winter a sketchbook waits on the sitting-room table at the Frostwood Lodge, and every guest adds a drawing. This year each drawing was written down in numbers, so anyone could draw it again.</b>

140 sketches wait inside, from the fireside to the mountaintop. Each one is a <b>nonogram</b> (also called hanjie or a paint-by-number grid). The numbers beside every row and above every column tell you which squares to shade, and a hidden picture appears: a cup of cocoa, a snowy cottage, an owl, a steam engine, a teapot and many more.

Every puzzle has <b>exactly one solution</b>, checked by computer, and every one can be finished line by line with <b>no guessing</b>.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>140 picture logic puzzles in four parts, in order of difficulty</li>
<li>30 Easy (15×15), two per page</li>
<li>40 Medium (20×20), one per page</li>
<li>40 Hard and 30 Expert (25×25), one per page</li>
<li>Clear grids with a bold line every five squares to help you count</li>
<li>A how-to-play guide with a fully worked example and the key solving tricks</li>
<li>Every finished picture, with its name, at the back</li>
<li>6 × 9 inch paperback, easy to carry and easy to write in</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations: a frontispiece of the lodge sitting room, a scene opening each section and small drawings on the puzzle pages</li>
</ul>

The sketches start indoors by the fire, then wander down to the village green, up through the pine woods and onto the high mountain. The grids grow from 15×15 to 25×25 as you go, and the later puzzles take more passes back and forth between the rows and columns.

Book six of the cozy Frostwood Puzzle Books. A good fit for adults, teens and families who enjoy nonograms, hanjie, logic puzzles, Sudoku and brain teasers, and a calm, screen-free winter gift.

Sharpen a pencil, pour some cocoa, and see what each sketch turns out to be.
```

## Why this puzzle type (researched 2026-10-06)
- Nonogram books sell steadily on Amazon: *The Big Book of Picross Hanjie Griddlers Nonograms* (DJAPE) 4.5★ from 77 reviews; *Nonogram Puzzle Book: From Beginner to Master* (Nov 2025) 4.5★ from 45 reviews at $19.99.
- Common complaints on cheaper titles: puzzles with errors or that need guessing (*Nonogram Puzzle Book for Adults: 100 Logic Puzzles*, 3.8★/19; *Nonograms Puzzle Books … 100 Puzzles*, 3.8★/21). This book's selling points answer that: every puzzle is checked unique and line-solvable with no guessing, and has a bold line every five squares.
- No cozy winter-themed nonogram book turned up in a quick search. The Frostwood series had no picture-logic book yet.
- Light Up (Akari) was considered first (fits the lodge-lantern theme) but dedicated Akari books have very few reviews (1–2 each), so it was not chosen.

## Picture source and credit
The puzzle pictures are made by rasterising glyphs of Google's monochrome **Noto Emoji** typeface (SIL Open Font License 1.1, copy of the licence in `src/fonts/NotoEmoji-OFL.txt`) onto 15×15, 20×20 and 25×25 grids. The OFL allows use in commercial works; the copyright page credits the typeface. Subjects were limited to cozy, non-violent, non-Christmas things (no people, no weapons, no religious buildings).

## Verification
Every puzzle was checked by an independent SAT solver (CaDiCaL via python-sat), which finds exactly one solution equal to the picture, and by the generator's line solver, which finishes it one row or column at a time with no guessing. Each fits its band (grid size, and the number of solver sweeps: Easy ≤ 9, Medium 3–12, Hard 6–13, Expert ≥ 14). Generation is deterministic (fixed settings and seeds; `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`), and a re-run produced a byte-identical `data.json`. See [verification.md](verification.md).
