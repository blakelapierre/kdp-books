# The Frostwood Puzzle Books (series)

Cozy winter puzzle books by **Blake La Pierre**, all set in and around the imaginary Frostwood: the old **Frostwood Lodge** at the top of the mountain, the **Frostwood Express** railway that climbs up to it, the **village** in the valley below, and the **mountain** itself. On KDP they could be linked as a series called **"A Frostwood Puzzle Book"** (every cover and title page already carries that line).

## Theme and rules for new books
- **Cozy and non-violent.** No murder, no violent crime, no gore. A theft or a puzzle to untangle is as dark as it gets.
- **Winter, but not Christmas-specific.** Snow, cocoa, fires, trains, skating, mountain huts. No Santa, no Christmas Eve countdowns, so the books sell from November through March.
- **Tied to the setting.** Each book's framing uses the lodge, the railway, the village or the mountain (station names, rooms, trails, bridges and so on).
- Each book uses a **different puzzle type**. Check the table below before choosing one.

## House style
- **Fonts** (all embedded, SIL Open Font License): **Crimson Text** (body text), **Playfair Display SC** (headings, titles, running heads), **IBM Plex Sans Condensed** (numbers, labels, grid clues). DejaVu Sans is used only for the ❄ bullet.
- **Interior:** 6 × 9 in, black ink on white paper, grayscale only, no bleed. Mirrored margins of 0.75 in inside, 0.55 in outside, 0.70 in top and 0.75 in bottom, all above KDP minimums. Snowflake dividers, "No. N" puzzle headers with 1 to 4 snowflakes for difficulty, band openers with a line-art vignette, and solutions at the back.
- **Cover:** full wrap with 0.125 in bleed. Navy night sky (#1f3a5f) with gold accents (#ffd27a), a slate mountain with snow caps, a snowy foreground, and drifting translucent snowflakes. Front has a small-caps kicker, a big Playfair SC title, a Crimson italic subtitle, a gold feature line, and "A Frostwood Puzzle Book" plus the author at the bottom. The back has a blurb, a feature list, a sample puzzle card and a blank 2 × 1.2 in barcode area in the lower right. Spine width is pages × 0.002252 in.
- **Front matter:** title page, copyright page ("Copyright © 2026 Blake La Pierre. All rights reserved.", no ISBN line), contents, how to play with a worked example. **Back matter:** thank-you page and "Also by Blake La Pierre".
- **Verification:** every puzzle is checked in code by a solver that is independent of the generator (exactly one solution, and it fits its difficulty band). The results go in `verification.md`, or `clue-checks.md` for book 1.
