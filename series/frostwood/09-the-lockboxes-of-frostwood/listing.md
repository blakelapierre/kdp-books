## Description
```html
<b>Snow on the pines. A brass lockbox on the boot-room bench. And a scrap of paper with three wrong guesses.</b>

Every winter, the keepers of Frostwood Lodge hide little lockboxes around the lodge, the village, the railway and the mountain. Guests have already tried a few codes on each one, and the lock told them how close they came. Put their notes together and crack the code.

120 crack-the-code logic puzzles, written for your phone. Nothing to write in. Tap <b>Need a hint?</b> to see one digit, or <b>Show answer</b> for the code with a check of every note, then tap back to the box.

Every puzzle has <b>exactly one solution</b>, and every note is needed, checked by two independent computer solvers.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>120 lockboxes in four bands: 30 Easy (the Lodge), 35 Medium (the Village), 35 Hard (the Railway), 20 Expert (the Mountain)</li>
<li>Three-digit locks to start, working up to five-digit mountain locks</li>
<li>Plain, exact clues like “One digit is right but in the wrong place”</li>
<li>A how-to-play guide with a step-by-step worked example</li>
<li>A one-digit hint for every box, and full answers that check every note</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations of Frostwood throughout</li>
</ul>

Book nine of the Frostwood Puzzle Books. Cozy, non-violent and not tied to any holiday, so it suits the whole winter. A good fit for adults, teens and families who like brain teasers, logic puzzles and code-breaking.

Pour some cocoa. The first box is in the boot room.
```
