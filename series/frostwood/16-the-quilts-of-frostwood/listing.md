## Why Shikaku
Shikaku (also sold as Rectangles or Divide by Squares) is a classic Nikoli grid puzzle that no earlier Frostwood book, standalone book or *Advent Clock* door uses, so it is a new type, not a sequel. It suits a giftable hardcover keepsake: the solved grids literally look like patchwork quilts, so the theme (Frostwood's winter quilting, from the village sewing circle to the heirloom quilts in the mountain huts) and the puzzle are the same picture. Solutions are printed as shaded patchwork, which makes the back of the book pleasant to browse.
