## 2. Upload the revised files (live book)

1. Open **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf**.
2. Find *The Thief Stayed the Night* → under **Paperback actions** tap **“…”** → **Edit paperback content**.
3. **Manuscript** → **Upload paperback manuscript** → choose `frostwood-01-the-thief-stayed-the-night-interior.pdf`.
4. Leave ink/paper (**Black & white interior, white paper**), trim (**6 × 9 in**) and bleed (**No bleed**) as they are.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → choose `frostwood-01-the-thief-stayed-the-night-cover.pdf`.
6. Tap **Launch Previewer**. Wait for it to load, page through (check the items in section 5), then **Approve**.
7. **Save and Continue** → on **Pricing**, check the printing cost now shows about **$2.63** and the royalty about **$3.36** at $9.99.
8. Tap **Publish Your Paperback Book**. The revision goes into review (up to 72 hours). The current version stays on sale while it's in review.

### Remove the “Grok Bot” co-author

1. Bookshelf → **“…”** → **Edit paperback details**.
2. Under **Contributors**, delete the **Grok Bot** row (tap the **×** / *Remove*). The **Author** must read **Blake La Pierre** only.
3. **Save and Continue** through Content and Pricing → **Publish**. (Contributors can be changed after publishing. The primary **Author** field is locked after publication: if “Grok Bot” is in that field rather than under Contributors, KDP Support can't change it and it would take a new edition.)
4. You can do this in the same session as the file upload: change the details first, then the content, then publish once.

<b>The road is gone. The snowplough is a week away. And things keep going missing.</b>

When a blizzard seals off the Frostwood Lodge, its 152 guests settle in for a week of cocoa, skating and cake. Then Mrs. Brisket's secret cocoa tin vanishes. Then the snowman's antique top hat. Then Lady Winifred's famous Starlight Sapphire disappears from her bedside table in the middle of the night.

With a developer circling to buy the old lodge and knock it down, the manager needs a detective, fast. That's you.

<b>The Thief Stayed the Night</b> is a cozy elimination-mystery puzzle book: twelve connected cases set over one snowbound week. Each case gives you a short story, a guest register listing every suspect, and a set of clues. Cross suspects off one by one until only the culprit remains. Every case has exactly one answer, and every clue is fair and self-contained: if a clue mentions "snowy cities" or "everyone at the quiz", it lists every one of them.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>

<li>A difficulty curve from 40 suspects and 6 clues up to all 152 guests and 14 clues</li>
<li>Later cases add room, floor, neighbour and roommate logic, with either/or and if/then clues</li>
<li>A light story running through all twelve cases, with a warm ending</li>
<li>A how-to-solve guide and a floor plan of the lodge</li>
<li>Verdict and notes pages for every case</li>
<li>Hints for when you're stuck, and full step-by-step solutions showing how many suspects each clue clears</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations: a frontispiece of the snowbound lodge and a drawing of each case's missing treasure</li>
</ul>

<b>No violence, ever.</b> The worst crimes at Frostwood are pranks, pinched pastries, a sabotaged Bake-Off sponge and one very suspicious painting. That makes it a good fit for teens, adults and families who enjoy puzzling together.

Grab a pencil, pour some cocoa, and see if you can find who did it before the snowplough arrives.

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **136** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] The frontispiece (page 2) and title-page art print as crisp black ink: solid blacks are even, no gray haze, no banding.
- [ ] Small vignettes never touch a puzzle, a grid, a clue or the page number, and nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge.
- [ ] Blank pages show only the small centred vignette; section openers start on right-hand pages.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
- [ ] Register tables (smallest text 6.8–7.6 pt) are readable; the gray row shading is light enough to write on.
- [ ] Each case's Verdict page vignette sits below the writing lines and does not crowd them.
