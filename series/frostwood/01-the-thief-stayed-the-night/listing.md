```html
<b>The road is gone. The snowplough is a week away. And things keep going missing.</b>

When a blizzard seals off the Frostwood Lodge, its 152 guests settle in for a week of cocoa, skating and cake. Then Mrs. Brisket's secret cocoa tin vanishes. Then the snowman's antique top hat. Then Lady Winifred's famous Starlight Sapphire disappears from her bedside table in the middle of the night.

With a developer circling to buy the old lodge and knock it down, the manager needs a detective, fast. That's you.

<b>The Thief Stayed the Night</b> is a cozy elimination-mystery puzzle book: twelve connected cases set over one snowbound week. Each case gives you a short story, a guest register listing every suspect, and a set of clues. Cross suspects off one by one until only the culprit remains. Every case has exactly one answer, and every clue is fair and self-contained: if a clue mentions "snowy cities" or "everyone at the quiz", it lists every one of them.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>12 cozy cases, from a missing cocoa tin to a forged portrait of the lodge's founder</li>
<li>A difficulty curve from 40 suspects and 6 clues up to all 152 guests and 14 clues</li>
<li>Later cases add room, floor, neighbour and roommate logic, with either/or and if/then clues</li>
<li>A light story running through all twelve cases, with a warm ending</li>
<li>A how-to-solve guide and a floor plan of the lodge</li>
<li>Verdict and notes pages for every case</li>
<li>Hints for when you're stuck, and full step-by-step solutions showing how many suspects each clue clears</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations: a frontispiece of the snowbound lodge and a drawing of each case's missing treasure</li>
</ul>

<b>No violence, ever.</b> The worst crimes at Frostwood are pranks, pinched pastries, a sabotaged Bake-Off sponge and one very suspicious painting. That makes it a good fit for teens, adults and families who enjoy puzzling together.

Grab a pencil, pour some cocoa, and see if you can find who did it before the snowplough arrives.
```

## Estimated royalty per copy (Amazon.com, standard distribution)
From KDP's published formula for black-ink paperbacks, regular trim, 110–828 pages, Amazon.com: fixed cost **$1.00** plus **$0.012 per page**.

- Printing cost = $1.00 + 136 × $0.012 = **$2.632 ≈ $2.63**
- Royalty = 60% × list price − printing cost
- At **$9.99**: 0.60 × 9.99 − 2.632 = **$3.36 per copy**

Other price points for comparison:

Minimum list price at 60% = 2.632 / 0.60 = $4.39. Since that is under $9.99, the 50% rate applies to any price below $9.99.
