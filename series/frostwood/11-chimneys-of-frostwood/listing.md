## Why Skyscrapers
Skyscrapers (also sold as Towers or Skyline puzzles) is a classic Latin-square logic puzzle that no earlier Frostwood book uses. *The Advent Clock* had one Skyscrapers door, which is fine. It suits a hardcover because the grids are small and calm (5×5 to 7×7), and it fits the setting: on a winter night every chimney in the village is lit, and a sign at the end of each lane counts the ones you can see.
