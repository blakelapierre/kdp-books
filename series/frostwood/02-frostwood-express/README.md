## 2. Upload the revised files (live book)

1. Open **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf**.
2. Find *Frostwood Express* → under **Paperback actions** tap **“…”** → **Edit paperback content**.
3. **Manuscript** → **Upload paperback manuscript** → choose `frostwood-02-frostwood-express-interior.pdf`.
4. Leave ink/paper (**Black & white interior, white paper**), trim (**6 × 9 in**) and bleed (**No bleed**) as they are.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → choose `frostwood-02-frostwood-express-cover.pdf`.
6. Tap **Launch Previewer**. Wait for it to load, page through (check the items in section 5), then **Approve**.
7. **Save and Continue** → on **Pricing**, check the printing cost now shows about **$3.33** and the royalty about **$3.87** at $11.99.
8. Tap **Publish Your Paperback Book**. The revision goes into review (up to 72 hours). The current version stays on sale while it's in review.

### Remove the “Grok Bot” co-author

1. Bookshelf → **“…”** → **Edit paperback details**.
2. Under **Contributors**, delete the **Grok Bot** row (tap the **×** / *Remove*). The **Author** must read **Blake La Pierre** only.
3. **Save and Continue** through Content and Pricing → **Publish**. (Contributors can be changed after publishing. The primary **Author** field is locked after publication: if “Grok Bot” is in that field rather than under Contributors, KDP Support can't change it and it would take a new edition.)
4. You can do this in the same session as the file upload: change the details first, then the content, then publish once.

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **194** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] The frontispiece (page 2) and title-page art print as crisp black ink: solid blacks are even, no gray haze, no banding.
- [ ] Small vignettes never touch a puzzle, a grid, a clue or the page number, and nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge.
- [ ] Blank pages show only the small centred vignette; section openers start on right-hand pages.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
- [ ] Pre-laid track pieces (rails and sleepers) are clear; the smallest solution grids (12×12) are readable.
- [ ] The vignettes under the Hard/Expert puzzles clear the B exit label and the Started/Finished line.
