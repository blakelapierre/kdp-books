## Description
```html
<b>Snow on the pines. A cocoa by the fire. And somebody in this room is fibbing.</b>

On snowy evenings at Frostwood Lodge, someone fetches the old tin marked GAME. Everyone draws a pinecone in secret: a snowy one makes you a truth-teller, a bare one makes you a fibber. Then each player says one thing. Truth-tellers only say true things, fibbers only say false things, and you have to work out who drew which.

120 truth-teller and fibber puzzles (the classic "knights and knaves" logic puzzle), written for your phone. Nothing to write in. Tap <b>Need a hint?</b> for a first step, or <b>Show answer</b> to see who drew which pinecone with a check of every statement, then tap back to the game.

Every puzzle has <b>exactly one solution</b>, and every statement is needed, checked by a computer solver that is separate from the one that made the puzzles.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>120 games in four bands: 30 Easy (the Lodge), 35 Medium (the Village), 35 Hard (the Railway), 20 Expert (the Mountain)</li>
<li>Three players to start, working up to six-player games on the mountain</li>
<li>Short, plain statements like “Greta and I are the same kind” and “Exactly two of us four are fibbers”</li>
<li>A how-to-play guide with a step-by-step worked example</li>
<li>A hint for every game, and full answers that check every statement</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations of Frostwood throughout</li>
</ul>

Book thirteen of the Frostwood Puzzle Books. Cozy, non-violent and not tied to any holiday, so it suits the whole winter. A good fit for adults, teens and families who like brain teasers, logic puzzles and whodunit-style deduction.

Pour some cocoa. The first game is by the great hearth.
```
