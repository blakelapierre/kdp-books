## 2. Create the paperback in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Paperback**.
2. Fill in **Paperback Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Paperback Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**; Publication date → leave blank; Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `frostwood-07-bridges-of-frostwood-interior.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `frostwood-07-bridges-of-frostwood-cover.pdf`.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Paperback Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**; Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**; list price **$9.99** (60% royalty, about $2.74/copy). Expanded Distribution is optional (40% rate).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **188** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.

- [ ] Easy and Medium pages: both puzzles fit on the page with clear space between them, and nothing touches the running head or page number.

## Verification

All 180 puzzles (and the worked example): exactly one solution, found by both an independent SAT solver (CaDiCaL via python-sat, with connectivity cuts) and an independent plain backtracking counter, and equal to the stored answer. Each fits its band: Easy needs only island counting, Medium also needs the network rule, Hard needs 1 to 3 one-step "what if?" deductions and Expert 2 to 6. Generation is deterministic (fixed seeds, `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`); a second run produced a byte-identical `data.json`. Full report: [verification.md](verification.md).
