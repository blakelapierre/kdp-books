## Why Slitherlink, and the competition
Slitherlink was evaluated for Frostwood book 3 on 2026-09-30 and passed over for Star Battle (top dedicated title 23 ratings). It was chosen for book 8 because it is the one classic loop puzzle not yet used in the series, and the theme fits Frostwood well: the lot fences are buried in snow and only the numbered posts show. All are bulk paperbacks (often four puzzles per page, plain grids); none is themed, illustrated or hardcover, and none shows a worked example or says how its puzzles were checked. Demand for dedicated Slitherlink books is thin, so this edition is positioned as a **giftable illustrated hardcover** at the same $17.99 price as *Stories We Keep* and *The Advent Clock* hardcover, rather than competing on puzzle count. **Gap:** a cozy, illustrated, Frostwood-themed Slitherlink hardcover with big clear maps, a worked example, a smooth difficulty curve and computer-verified unique, no-guess puzzles.

## Royalty math (from the actual 88-page interior)
KDP black-ink hardcover printing, regular trim, Amazon.com, **75–108 pages: $6.80 fixed, no per-page cost** (KDP Help, [Hardcover Printing Cost](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H), fetched 2026-10-15). 88 pages is inside that band.
Hardcover royalty at 60%: **0.60 × $17.99 − $6.80 = $10.794 − $6.80 = $3.994 ≈ $3.99** per copy sold on Amazon.com (other marketplaces differ).
If the page count ever rose to 110 or more, the cost would become $5.65 + $0.012 × pages instead.

## Verification
Every puzzle was checked by an independent SAT solver (`src/slither_sat.py`: CaDiCaL via python-sat, with lazily added single-loop cuts) and by an independent backtracking counter (`src/slither_bt.py`, which shares no code with the generator). All 100 puzzles, plus the worked example, have exactly one solution, and it matches the stored fence. Each is solvable by logic alone and fits its band, graded by the generator's step-by-step logic solver: Easy (6×6) is solved by the counting rules alone; Medium (7×7) also needs short what-ifs (a guess that breaks within 8 forced moves); Hard (8×8) needs 1–3 long what-ifs; Expert (10×10) needs 3–12. As a cross-check, the two counters were also run on a copy of each puzzle with one number removed and agreed every time. Generation is deterministic (fixed seeds; `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`), and a second run produced a byte-identical `data.json`. See [verification.md](verification.md).
