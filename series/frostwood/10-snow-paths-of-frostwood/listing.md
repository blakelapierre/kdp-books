## Why Nurikabe, and the competition
Nurikabe had not been used anywhere in the catalogue (Frostwood or standalone). It fits Frostwood well: the shaded path is the path the village digs through deep snow, and the numbered patches are the untouched snow left between. Dedicated Nurikabe books on Amazon are few and thinly reviewed. Public Amazon listings seen in a web search on 2026-10-27 (figures as shown in the search results; nothing else was checked):

Sales ranks weren't shown for these, so demand is unmeasured; the low review counts suggest a small niche, like Hashi and Slitherlink. **Gap:** a cozy, illustrated, themed Nurikabe book with a smooth Easy-to-Expert curve (7×7 up to 12×12), a worked example, and computer-verified unique, no-guess puzzles at $9.99.

## Print specs used
- Trim 6 × 9 in (regular trim), black ink on **white** paper, no bleed interior, matte cover
- Interior: **160 pages** (includes the black-and-white ink illustrations: solid black line art on white, 300 DPI, no gray washes), grayscale only, all fonts embedded and subset (Crimson Text, Playfair Display SC, IBM Plex Sans Condensed, and DejaVu Sans for the ❄ symbol). Mirrored margins: inside 0.75 in, outside 0.55 in, top 0.70 in, bottom 0.75 in, above KDP's minimums.
- Layout: Easy (7×7) and Medium (9×9) two per page; Hard (10×10) and Expert (12×12) one per page with a vignette and a Started / Finished line. Solutions: 12 per page (Easy, Medium), 6 per page (Hard, Expert), path shaded gray.
- Spine width: 160 × 0.002252 in = **0.3603 in** (spine text is allowed; KDP recommends 79+ pages)
- Full-wrap cover: 0.125 + 6 + 0.3603 + 6 + 0.125 = **12.6103 in × 9.25 in**. The barcode area (2 × 1.2 in, lower right of the back cover, 0.25 in in from the trim) has no text or art.

## Suggested price
**$9.99 USD** (Amazon.com), the lowest price that earns the 60% royalty rate, and the same price as the Djape and Logic Grid Press Nurikabe books.

## Royalty math (from the actual 160-page interior)
KDP black-ink printing for white paper: **$1.00 + 160 × $0.012 = $2.920** per copy.
Paperback royalty at 60%: **0.60 × $9.99 − $2.920 = $3.07** per copy sold on Amazon.com (expanded distribution and other marketplaces pay less).

## Verification
Every puzzle was checked by an independent SAT solver (CaDiCaL via python-sat, with lazily added connectivity cuts) and by an independent backtracking counter (`src/nk_bt.py`, which shares no code with the generator). All 160 puzzles, plus the worked example, have exactly one solution, and it matches the stored answer. Each fits its band, graded by the generator's step-by-step logic solver (`src/nurikabe.py`): Easy needs no what-if steps, Medium needs exactly the "list the ways" tier, Hard needs 1–6 what-if steps and Expert 4–38. Full report: [verification.md](verification.md).
