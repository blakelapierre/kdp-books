## Description
```html
<b>Five pegs in the boot room. Five snowy scarves. Who hung theirs where?</b>

Everything at Frostwood comes in rows: pegs by the lodge door, stalls at the winter market, seats in the observation car, sledges at the top of Long Hill. In every puzzle a few friends are lined up side by side, a handful of short clues tell you about the row, and you work out exactly who has which place.

120 line-up logic puzzles, written for your phone. Nothing to write in. Tap <b>Need a hint?</b> for a first step, or <b>Show answer</b> to see the line-up with every clue ticked off, then tap back to the puzzle.

Every puzzle has <b>exactly one solution</b>, and every clue is needed, checked by a computer solver that is separate from the one that made the puzzles.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>120 puzzles in four bands: 30 Easy (the Lodge), 30 Medium (the Village), 30 Hard (the Railway), 30 Expert (the Mountain)</li>
<li>Four people in a row to start, working up to seven on the mountain</li>
<li>Short, plain clues like “Greta is right next to Finn” and “There are exactly two seats between Ada and Bram”</li>
<li>A how-to-play guide with a step-by-step worked example</li>
<li>A hint for every puzzle, and full answers that check every clue</li>
<li>Black-and-white ink illustrations of Frostwood throughout</li>
</ul>

Book fifteen of the Frostwood Puzzle Books. Cozy, non-violent and not tied to any holiday, so it suits the whole winter. A good fit for adults, teens and families who like brain teasers, logic puzzles and seating puzzles.

Kick the snow off your boots. The first row of pegs is just inside the door.
```
