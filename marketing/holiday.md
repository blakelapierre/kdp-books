## 1. The Advent Clock launch checklist

**Goal:** proof in hand by about **Oct 20**, live by **Nov 1**, and **Nov 8 at the latest**. Ads ramp **Nov 15–Dec 15**.
The book is used Dec 1–24, so every week it's live before December counts.

### Now to Oct 9: set up the title
- [ ] Final check of `standalone/the-advent-clock/interior.pdf` and `cover.pdf` (89 pages, 6 × 9 in).
- [ ] On the KDP Bookshelf, create a new **paperback** title. Paste the title, subtitle, description, keywords and categories from `standalone/the-advent-clock/listing.md`.
- [ ] Upload the interior and cover. Approve them in the **Print Previewer**, then save as a draft.
- [ ] Author field: **Blake La Pierre** only. Check the Contributors list, because the live books picked up an extra co-author. See [author-central.md](author-central.md).
- [ ] Set the price to **$9.99** (royalty about $3.69).

### Oct 9–12: order the proof
- [ ] Bookshelf → **"…"** next to the draft → **Request Printed Proofs**. You can order up to 5.
- [ ] Watch for the checkout email, which arrives within **4 hours**. Complete checkout within **24 hours**.
- [ ] Check the delivery estimate at checkout. If it's later than **Oct 20**, pick faster shipping.
  - Sources: KDP Help, *Proofs and author copies*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVEG4YA9G2T7N6DR, and *Proof delivery*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GL9S534PE857E2CN

### Oct 20: check the proof
- [ ] Cover: title readable, spine centered, no text cut off.
- [ ] The Door Log and letter boxes are big enough to write in.
- [ ] Mazes, pigpen symbols and small grids print clearly.
- [ ] Hints (p59–63) and solutions (from p65) match their doors.
- [ ] Solve two or three doors with a pencil to check they're comfortable to use.

### Oct 21–23: publish
- [ ] Fix anything the proof showed, then re-approve in the Previewer.
- [ ] Click **Publish**. A paperback can take **up to 72 hours** to go live. Source: KDP *Timelines*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620
- Option: KDP can **schedule a release** up to 90 days ahead for new print books. The Amazon page stays hidden until release day, and files lock 5 days before. There are **no print pre-orders**. For this book, just publish as soon as it's ready. Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTMGBCWHAY35NK4

### When live (target Nov 1, deadline Nov 8)
- [ ] Open the Amazon page and check the price, description and "Look inside" when it appears.
- [ ] Claim it in Author Central ([author-central.md](author-central.md)).
- [ ] Submit A+ Content ([a-plus/the-advent-clock/](a-plus/the-advent-clock/module-text.md)). Approval can take up to 8 business days.
- [ ] Launch the three ad campaigns at **$5/day** in total ([amazon-ads.md §5.3](amazon-ads.md#53-the-advent-clock-prepared)).
- [ ] Post the free sample (Doors One and Two) on Pinterest, Facebook groups and r/printables ([social/](social/posts.md)).
- [ ] Order a few **author copies** for local gifts or ARCs. The Order Author Copies button appears once the book is live.

### Nov 15–Dec 15: ramp the ads
- [ ] Raise the Advent campaigns to **$10/day** in total.
- [ ] Do the weekly check every Monday ([amazon-ads.md §4](amazon-ads.md#4-weekly-optimization-checklist)).
- [ ] Nov 27–30 (Black Friday to Cyber Monday): keep budgets at $10. Don't cut during this peak.
- [ ] Dec 1: post "Door One opens today!" People who start late can still catch up.

### Dec 16–21: wind down
- [ ] Dec 16: drop to **$5/day**.
- [ ] About **Dec 21**: pause the Advent campaigns. Amazon's delivery dates at checkout show whether orders can still arrive in time. Check them rather than guessing.
- [ ] Jan: note what worked for next year's relaunch (it's evergreen each November).

## 4. Pricing notes

- **Kindle Countdown Deals don't apply.** They're only for **Kindle eBooks enrolled in KDP Select**. These books are **paperbacks**, so there's no Countdown Deal or Free Book Promotion for them. Source: KDP Help, *Kindle Countdown Deals*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201293780
- **Series discounts** aren't available either. Source: KDP Help, *Series*, https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GMFKBUS43QQ5AJ5A
- The only paperback price lever is to **change the list price yourself**. It takes **72 hours to 5 business days** to show (KDP *Timelines*).
- **Don't go below $9.99.** Under $9.99 the paperback royalty rate falls from 60% to 50%, so a $1 cut costs much more than $1 in royalty.

  | Book | Royalty at $9.99 | Royalty at $8.99 (about) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Thief | $3.39 | $1.89 |
  | Express | $2.69 | $1.19 |
  | Advent | $3.69 | $2.19 |
  | Stars | $2.81 | $1.31 |

  The $8.99 column is 50% × $8.99 minus the same printing cost.
- **Frostwood Express is live at $11.99** (royalty about $3.89). All four books match at $9.99 for gift shopping. If you want that, change the price by **Nov 10** so it's showing before the Nov 15 push. If you keep $11.99, use the 32.4% break-even ACoS in the ads plan.
- **Don't put prices or "sale" wording in A+ Content.** It isn't allowed.
