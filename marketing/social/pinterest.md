# Pinterest Pins

Back to the [marketing kit](../README.md) · Images are in [pinterest/](pinterest/). They're 1000 × 1500 px (2:3) and rebuilt with `marketing/src/build_pins.py`.

**Links**
- **Pin 1** (cover pin) links to the book's **Amazon page**.
- **Pin 2** (puzzle pin) links to the **free printable sample**.
- The sample PDFs are in a private GitHub repo, so upload them somewhere public first. Options include a Google Drive file set to "Anyone with the link", your own site, or a Dropbox link. Then replace `[SAMPLE-PDF-LINK: …]`.
- Only pin *Stars over Frostwood* and *The Advent Clock* **after they're live**.

**Boards to create**
- Printable Logic Puzzles
- Cozy Winter Puzzle Books
- Puzzle Gifts & Stocking Stuffers (use this one from Nov 1)
- Printable Advent Activities (use this one from Nov 1)
