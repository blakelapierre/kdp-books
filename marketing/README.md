## The five pillars

1. **Early reviews, done by the rules.** Family and close friends may buy and share, but **not review**. Offer free copies with no review required. → [reviews.md](reviews.md)
2. **Sponsored Products ads.** Each book gets an auto, a keyword and a product campaign, at $5–10/day per book, with weekly rules. → [amazon-ads.md](amazon-ads.md)
3. **Listing polish.** Claim the books in Author Central, add the bio, link Frostwood books 1–3, and add A+ Content. → [author-central.md](author-central.md) and [a-plus/](a-plus/README.md)
4. **Holiday timing.** Advent live by Nov 1–8, ads ramp Nov 15–Dec 15, then shift to the Frostwood winter books. → [holiday.md](holiday.md)
5. **Free printable samples.** Lead every post with a real puzzle and its answers. → [social/](social/posts.md)

## Ground rules used in this kit
- Cozy and non-violent throughout.
- **No invented statistics or reviews.** Bids and conversion rates are marked as starting assumptions.
- Everything about Amazon or KDP cites a public help page.
- Nobody signed in to Amazon, KDP or Author Central to make this kit. The listing facts come from public pages, as seen on Sep 30 2026.
