## Author bios

These only use facts that are true for every book. **Don't add credentials, awards or sales claims.** If you want to add something personal, like where you live or why you started, keep it true.

### Short (about 25 words)
> Blake La Pierre is an independent puzzle maker who writes cozy, non-violent puzzle books. Every puzzle is computer-checked to have exactly one solution.

### Medium (about 60 words)
> Blake La Pierre is an independent puzzle maker. Blake's books are cozy and gentle: snowy lodges, mountain railways, starry winter skies and mysteries with no violence at all. Every puzzle is checked by a computer program to make sure it has exactly one solution, so there's no guessing and no broken pages. Just a pencil, a warm drink and a fair challenge.

### Long (about 130 words)
> Blake La Pierre is an independent puzzle maker who creates cozy, non-violent puzzle books for adults and teens.
>
> The Frostwood Puzzle Books are set in a snowy mountain village. *The Thief Stayed the Night* is a set of elimination mysteries in a snowbound hotel. *Frostwood Express* is a book of Train Tracks puzzles along a mountain railway. *Stars over Frostwood* has Star Battle puzzles under a winter sky. *The Advent Clock* is a standalone December countdown with a puzzle behind each of its twenty-four doors.
>
> Blake cares most about fairness. Every puzzle is checked by a computer program to have exactly one solution, and every book includes solutions, and hints where they help. There are no apps, no timers and nothing scary. Just clear logic, a pencil and a little winter calm.

> The bios avoid pronouns, so they work as written.

## Claim the books in Author Central

Source: KDP Help, *Author Central*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644310

1. In KDP, open the **Marketing** tab.
2. Under **Author Central**, choose the marketplace (Amazon.com) and click **Manage author page**.
3. Click **Join for free** and sign in with your KDP account.
4. Search for one of your books by **ISBN, ASIN or author name**. Use B0HLMMS675.
5. Confirm with **"Yes, join as Blake La Pierre"**, or the button that matches your name.
6. On the **Books** tab, add each book: **Add a Book** → search by title or ASIN → click the cover → **Add this book**. This is from KDP Help, *Amazon Ads*: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201499010. Books must be in Author Central before you can advertise them.
   - The Thief Stayed the Night: B0HLMMS675
   - Frostwood Express: B0HLMB966Q
   - Stars over Frostwood and The Advent Clock: add them once each one is live.
7. Add the **bio** (medium or long above) and an author photo if you want one.
8. **Editorial reviews** (US): add only real, attributable quotes that you have permission to use. Leave this empty for now rather than inventing anything.

Author Central's own help pages (author.amazon.com) need JavaScript and couldn't be read for this kit. If a button name differs, follow the nearest match on screen.

## ⚠️ Fix the co-author credit

The public pages for **both live books** (checked Sep 30 2026) list **"Blake La Pierre (Author), Grok Bot (Author)"**. If that isn't what you want:

- KDP says **contributors can be changed after publishing, but the primary author can't**. Source: KDP Help, *Authors and Contributors*, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G2BWJN2BY98T5PV2
- On your **Bookshelf**, open the book's paperback **details** and remove the extra entry in **Contributors**. Then save and republish the details. Changes can take up to 72 hours.
- Do this **before** claiming the books in Author Central. Otherwise the Grok Bot byline may appear on your author page.

## Link the Frostwood series

Amazon already shows *The Thief Stayed the Night* and *Frostwood Express* as **"Book 1 of 2"** and **"Book 2 of 2"** (public page, Sep 30 2026). So the series exists. You only need to add *Stars over Frostwood* as **Book 3** once it's live.

Sources:
- KDP Help, *Series*: https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/GMFKBUS43QQ5AJ5A
- KDP Help, *Manage series*: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G6PVAHKQS87GVK6A

**Add Stars over Frostwood to the series**
1. On the **Bookshelf**, click the **"…"** button next to *Stars over Frostwood*.
2. Choose **Add to series**.
3. Pick the existing Frostwood series and set its order to **3**.
4. Save. Series page updates can take **up to 72 hours** (KDP Timelines, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202173620).

**Check or edit the series**
- On the Bookshelf, use the book's **Manage series** link, or **"…"** → **Manage series**.
- You can also create a series from **+ Create new title or series** → **Create series page**. This isn't needed here.
- Keep the series name the same on every book. The listings use **A Frostwood Puzzle Book**.

**Don't add The Advent Clock** to the series. It's a standalone book, set at Snowberry Lodge.
