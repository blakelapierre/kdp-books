# Early Reviews: Rules, Messages and Sources

Back to the [marketing kit](README.md).

Reviews matter most in the first weeks. They're also where it's easiest to break Amazon's rules without meaning to. Read the rules first.

---

## ⚠️ One correction first

The kit brief assumed that friends and family may review if they bought the book. **Amazon's public rules say they may not.** Buying the book doesn't change that.

- Amazon's **Promotional Content** page lists this as not allowed: *"A relative, close friend, business associate, or employee of the product creator posts a review to help boost sales."*
  https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202094170
- Amazon's **Community Guidelines** say you may not *"post reviews on products from sellers, authors, brands, or artists that you have a personal connection with."*
  https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF

What friends and family **can** do is buy the book, gift it, and tell their own friends about it. They must say how they know you, for example *"Check out my brother's new puzzle book!"* The Community Guidelines give this kind of disclosed promotion as an allowed example.

---

## The rules in plain words

**Never allowed**
- Paying for a review, in money, gift cards, refunds or anything else.
- Review swaps ("I'll review yours if you review mine").
- Requiring a review in exchange for a free copy.
- Asking for a *positive* review, or offering something only to people who leave good reviews.
- Reviews from relatives, close friends, business associates or employees.
- Review clubs and review groups.
- Writing a review of your own book.

**Allowed**
- Giving someone a free or discounted copy, **if no review is required** and you don't try to influence what they say. Source: KDP Customer Reviews, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202101910
- Advance review copies (ARCs) from the author, under the same conditions. The Community Guidelines allow this.
- Politely asking customers for **honest** feedback, e.g. *"Please share your honest feedback."*

**Good to know**
- To post a review, someone needs to have spent at least $50 on Amazon.com in the past 12 months (Community Guidelines).
- Amazon can remove reviews it thinks break the rules, and can take action against accounts.
- Never reply to reviews with pressure or arguments. A kind thank-you in your own channels is fine.

## Message 1: Close friends and family (share, don't review)

> Hi [Name]! My puzzle book **[Title]** is now out on Amazon: [Amazon link]
>
> It's a cozy winter puzzle book, and every puzzle has been checked by computer to have exactly one solution. If you'd enjoy it, or know someone who would, I'd love it if you picked up a copy or gave one as a gift.
>
> One small thing: Amazon doesn't allow reviews from family and close friends, so please **don't** leave a review. Sharing it with your friends helps just as much. If you post about it, just mention that I'm your [brother / friend / etc.].
>
> Thank you so much! 💙 Blake

---

## Message 2: Wider circle (honest review welcome, never required)

Use this for people who aren't close to you. Examples: someone in a puzzle group who tried the free sample, a newsletter reader, or a customer who wrote to you.

> Hi [Name], thanks for trying the free sample of **[Title]**!
>
> If you'd like more, the full book is on Amazon: [Amazon link]
>
> If you do get it, I'd be grateful for your **honest** feedback in an Amazon review. Good or bad, it helps other puzzlers decide, and it helps me make the next book better. No pressure at all.
>
> Happy puzzling! Blake

---

## Message 3: ARC offer (free copy, no review required)

> Hi [Name], I'm looking for a few puzzle fans to read an advance copy of **[Title]**, [one-line description].
>
> I'll send you a copy for free. **You're under no obligation to review it**, and the copy is yours to keep either way. If you do choose to review it on Amazon, please be completely honest. It's good practice to mention that you got a free copy.
>
> Would you like one?
>
> Blake

Tips for ARCs
- Only send ARCs to people you **don't** have a close personal connection with.
- Send the same message to everyone. Don't follow up asking "did you review yet?"
- For paperbacks, send a printed author copy. KDP lets you order these once the book is live. Or send the free printable sample in `social/samples/`.
- Never send a gift, refund or extra copy after someone reviews.

## Where to find honest readers

| Source | OK? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzle groups on Facebook, Reddit and Discord | ✅ If the group's rules allow promotion | Lead with the **free printable sample**. See [social/posts.md](social/posts.md). Never trade reviews inside a group. |
| Your own ARC list (newsletter signup, sample downloaders) | ✅ | Use Message 3. No review required. |
| Local puzzle clubs, libraries, board-game cafés | ✅ | Give a copy or a sample. If you know them personally, they shouldn't review. |
| Pinterest and Instagram followers who print the sample | ✅ | Use Message 2 only when they reply to you. |
| Amazon Vine or seller "Request a Review" tools | ⚠️ Not checked | These are Seller Central tools for brand owners. I couldn't confirm that KDP paperbacks can use them, so don't plan around them. |
| Review swaps between authors | ❌ Against the rules | Explicitly banned on the Promotional Content page. |
| Paid review services ("50 reviews for $X") | ❌ Against the rules | Compensated reviews are not allowed. |
| "Review for a refund" or gift-card deals | ❌ Against the rules | Banned by Amazon and KDP. |
| Review clubs and "review exchange" groups | ❌ Against the rules | Named as not allowed in the Community Guidelines. |
| Family, close friends and coworkers | ❌ For reviews | They can buy and share with a disclosure, but not review. |
| Blake reviewing his own book | ❌ | Never. |

## Checklist

- [ ] Send Message 1 to close friends and family (share, no reviews).
- [ ] Post the free sample in 2–3 puzzle groups that allow it.
- [ ] Offer ARCs to 5–10 people who aren't personal connections (Message 3).
- [ ] Put a line at the end of the free sample: *"Enjoyed it? The full book is on Amazon."* It's already in the PDFs.
- [ ] Never offer anything in return for a review.
