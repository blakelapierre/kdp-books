## Module 1: Standard Image Header With Text
**Image:** `1-header-970x600.png`
**Alt text:** Cover of Stars over Frostwood beside a snowy night sky and the book's features

**Headline**
> Put the Stars Back in the Sky

**Subheadline**
> 180 Star Battle Logic Puzzles from Frostwood

**Body**
> On clear winter nights, the Frostwood Star Club climbs to the little observatory on the roof of the lodge, but the snow clouds have smudged every chart. Each patch of sky is a Star Battle puzzle, also called Two Not Touch. Place stars so that every row, every column, and every outlined region holds the same number, and no two stars touch, not even diagonally.
