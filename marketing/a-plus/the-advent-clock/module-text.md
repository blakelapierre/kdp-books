## Module 1: Standard Image Header With Text
**Image:** `1-header-970x600.png`
**Alt text:** Cover of The Advent Clock beside a snowy night sky and the book's features

**Headline**
> Twenty-Four Doors and One Hidden Star

**Subheadline**
> A Daily Puzzle Countdown at Snowberry Lodge

**Body**
> In the lobby of Snowberry Lodge stands a great clock with twenty-four little doors, and behind one of them the old clockmaker has hidden a silver star. Open one door a day. Behind each is a short, cozy story scene and a puzzle: a secret code, a snowy maze, a word grid, a logic problem, and more. Every answer gives you a key letter, and the twenty-four letters spell out where the star is hidden.

## Module 3: Standard Image Header With Text
**Image:** `3-why-these-puzzles-970x600.png`
**Alt text:** Why these puzzles: exactly one answer, three hint levels, cozy with no crime

**Headline**
> Why These Puzzles

**Body**
> Every puzzle was checked by a computer program to have exactly one answer, so no day is broken. Each door has three levels of hints (Nudge, Foothold, and Last Step), and full solutions come one per page with a short explanation. There's nothing scary and no crime to solve, and no phone, app, or internet is needed. Just a pencil.
