**Alt text:** Cover of The Thief Stayed the Night beside a snowy night sky and the book's features

**Headline**
> Twelve Cozy Cases in a Snowbound Hotel

**Subheadline**
> An Elimination Mystery Puzzle Book from Frostwood

**Body**
> A blizzard has sealed off the Frostwood Lodge, and its 152 guests are settling in for a week of cocoa, skating, and cake. Then things start to go missing. Each case gives you a short story, a guest register, and a set of clues. Cross off suspects one clue at a time until only the culprit remains. Twelve connected cases grow from 40 suspects and six clues to all 152 guests and 14 clues, with hints for when you're stuck and step-by-step solutions at the back.
