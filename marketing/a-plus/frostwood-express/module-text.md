## Module 1: Standard Image Header With Text
**Image:** `1-header-970x600.png`
**Alt text:** Cover of Frostwood Express beside a snowy night sky and the book's features

**Headline**
> Lay the Line and Climb the Mountain

**Subheadline**
> 200 Train Tracks Logic Puzzles from Frostwood

**Body**
> The snow came down all night, and the line to the mountain is buried. Two hundred stretches of track lie between the valley and the old lodge at the summit. In each Train Tracks puzzle, the numbers beside the rows and columns tell you how many squares hold track, and a few pieces are already in place. Lay one unbroken railway from A to B, with no branches, no crossings, and no loops.
