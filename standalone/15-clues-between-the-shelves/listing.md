## Description
```html
<b>Twenty-eight gentle mysteries from an independent bookshop. Can you solve them before the answer?</b>

Welcome to Willow Quill Books on Maple Lane, where Iris Quill and her helper Theo keep the kettle warm, the shelves tidy, and one eye on the little puzzles that walk through the door. A cookbook turns up in travel. A bookmark goes missing. Two tote bags are swapped. A special order ends up in the wrong hands. Quill the cat may be involved.

Each case is a short story with every clue in plain sight. At the end you hear <i>Can you solve it?</i>, a pause to think it over, and then the solution, explained clue by clue. Most cases turn on something somebody said or left behind. A few are little logic puzzles about seating, queues and fibs.

<b>Made for cozy evenings and listening:</b>
<ul>
<li>28 original solve-it-yourself bookshop mysteries (24 observation clues + 4 text-logic)</li>
<li>Each story is a few minutes long, perfect for a commute or a cup of tea</li>
<li>Written to be read aloud: no pictures, charts or grids, every clue is in the words</li>
<li>The answer comes right after every pause, so it works beautifully as an audiobook</li>
<li>Gentle and kind: missing books, mis-shelved clues, borrowed items, mixed-up orders and small fibs. Nobody is ever hurt</li>
<li>For adults who like cozy mysteries without murder</li>
</ul>

Open the door. The sign says Open, the kettle is on, and Iris has kept a case for you.
```
