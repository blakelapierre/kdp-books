## Description
```html
<b>Twenty-four little mysteries. One camper van. Can you solve them before the answer?</b>

June notices everything. Her little brother Theo asks about everything. Every summer they ride in the back of Biscuit, their grandparents' camper van, with Grandpa Gus at the wheel, Grandma Rosie with the map, and Pickles the dog asleep on somebody's feet. And wherever Biscuit stops, there is a small mystery waiting.

Who ate the last cinnamon roll? Where did Grandma's sunglasses go? Which jar is really the apricot jam? Who left the gift shop door open, and why does every footprint have a little dot beside it?

Each case is a short story with every clue in plain sight. At the end you hear <i>Can you solve it?</i>, a pause to think it over, and then the solution, explained clue by clue. No flipping to the back of the book.

<b>Made for car rides, bedtime and listening together:</b>
<ul>
<li>24 original solve-it-yourself mysteries, each about a three-minute read</li>
<li>Written to be read aloud: no pictures, charts or grids, every clue is in the words</li>
<li>The answer comes right after every pause, perfect for audiobooks</li>
<li>Gentle and kind: missing snacks, swapped labels and lost tickets. Nobody is ever hurt, and nothing is scary</li>
<li>Great for kids about seven to twelve, and grown-ups who like to guess too</li>
</ul>

Buckle up, keep your eyes open, and see if you can beat June to the answer.
```
