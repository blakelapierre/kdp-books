## 2. Create the paperback in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Paperback**.
2. Fill in **Paperback Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Paperback Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**; Publication date → leave blank; Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `standalone-04-logic-on-lantern-lane-interior.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `standalone-04-logic-on-lantern-lane-cover.pdf`.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Paperback Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**; Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**; list price **$9.99** (60% royalty, about $2.67/copy). Expanded Distribution is optional (40% rate, about $0.67/copy).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

Who brought the lemon tart, and what did it cost? Which skater drank cocoa after six laps? Whose snowman wore the tea cosy? ## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **194** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] The frontispiece (page 2) and title-page art print as crisp black ink: solid blacks are even, no gray haze, no banding.
- [ ] Grid labels (small sans-serif text beside and above each grid) are readable, and grid boxes are big enough for an X with a sharp pencil, especially on the Expert spreads.
- [ ] Every Expert puzzle opens as a two-page spread: clues on the left-hand page, grid and answer table on the right.
- [ ] Nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge; blank pages show only the small centred lamppost; section openers start on right-hand pages.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
