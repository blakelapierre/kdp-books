## Description
```html
<b>A little mystery for every night of December. Can you solve them before the answer?</b>

This Christmas, Wren and Felix are staying with Grandma Nell and Grandpa Walt above the little red-brick post office in the snowy village of Bellwick. Behind the counter hangs a wooden calendar with twenty-four doors. And every night, there is a small mystery to solve.

Who opened the first door early? Who wrote the letter to Santa with no name on it? Where did the snowman's carrot nose go? Why did the wreath end up on the wrong door? And who is leaving a paper snowflake with a single letter in the calendar every night?

Each night is a short story with every clue in plain sight. At the end you hear <i>Can you solve it?</i>, a pause to think it over, and then the solution, explained clue by clue. Collect the snowflake letters as you go, and on Christmas Eve they spell out a secret message.

<b>Made for cozy December evenings and listening together:</b>
<ul>
<li>24 original solve-it-yourself Christmas mysteries, one for each night from December first to Christmas Eve</li>
<li>Each story is about a three-minute read, perfect for bedtime</li>
<li>Written to be read aloud: no pictures, charts or grids, every clue is in the words</li>
<li>The answer comes right after every pause, so it works beautifully as an audiobook</li>
<li>Gentle and kind: lost mittens, mixed-up parcels and secret good deeds. Nobody is ever hurt, and nothing is scary</li>
<li>Great for kids about seven to twelve, and the grown-ups who like to guess too</li>
</ul>

Light the tree, open the first door, and see if you can beat Wren to the answer.
```

## Content
- **24** cozy Christmas mini-mysteries, one per night from December first to Christmas Eve, each ending *Can you solve it?*, then *Think about it...*, then the solution
- **12,562 words** total
- A running thread: each night ends with a snowflake letter; together they spell THANK YOU FOR EVERY KINDNESS, and Night Twenty-Four reveals who wrote them
- Nights: The Door That Opened Early; The Letter Without a Name; The Carrot Nose; The Two Parcels; The Wreath at Dawn; The Gumdrop Roof; The Card That Came Back; The Jingling in the Night; The Skates at the Pond; The Snowflake on the Bakery Window; The Open Gate; The Missing Post Box Key; The Frosty Car; The Smudged Thank-You Note; The Dog Who Didn't Bark; The Cookie Contest Tags; The Secret Santa Swap; The Star on the Village Tree; The Wreath on the Wrong Door; The Library Book in the Letterbox; The Footprints to the Shed; The Postcard from the North Pole; The Three Presents; Christmas Eve: The Snowflake Writer
- Non-violent: lost mittens, mixed-up parcels, secret good deeds. Nobody is hurt and there are no villains.
- Characters: Wren Hartley (ten), Felix (seven), Grandma Nell and Grandpa Walt, Marzipan the cat, and the villagers of Bellwick
