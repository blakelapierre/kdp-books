## Description
```html
<b>Light a candle, take three clues, and see if you can guess before the answer.</b>

In the imaginary village of Candlewick Hollow, friends gather at a cottage table for cozy What-Am-I rounds. Kitchen cupboards, herb gardens, rainy windows, bookstore shelves, firesides, sewing baskets, porches at dusk, market baskets, attic trunks and candlelit tables — one hundred fifty original riddles in ten themed rounds.

Every riddle gives you three clues (harder, then easier), a pause to think, then the answer and a short cozy fact. No flipping to the back of the book.

<b>Made for reading or listening:</b>
<ul>
<li>150 original three-clue What-Am-I riddles for adults and families</li>
<li>Ten cozy cottage themes with fifteen riddles each</li>
<li>The answer comes right after every pause</li>
<li>No grids, charts or pictures — every clue is in the words, so it works read aloud, in the car or at bedtime</li>
<li>Clean and kind. No violence — only ordinary cottage things to name</li>
</ul>

A lovely pick for fans of riddles, brain teasers and listen-and-guess games.
```
