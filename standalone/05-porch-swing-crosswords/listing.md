## Estimated royalty per copy (Amazon.com)
From KDP's published formula for black-ink paperbacks, regular trim, 110–828 pages, Amazon.com: fixed cost **$1.00** plus **$0.012 per page**. (For 24–110 pages the flat rate is $2.30; this book is above that band.)

- Printing cost = $1.00 + 196 × $0.012 = **$3.352 ≈ $3.35**
- Royalty = 60% × list price − printing cost
- At **$9.99**: 0.60 × 9.99 − 3.352 = 5.994 − 3.352 = **$2.64 per copy**
