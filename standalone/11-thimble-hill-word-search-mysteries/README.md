## 2. Create the paperback in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Paperback**.
2. Fill in **Paperback Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Paperback Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**; Publication date → leave blank; Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `interior-standalone-11-thimble-hill-word-search-mysteries.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `cover-standalone-11-thimble-hill-word-search-mysteries.pdf`.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Paperback Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**; Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**; list price **$9.99** (60% royalty, about $3.58/copy). Expanded Distribution is optional (40% rate, about $1.58/copy).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

## 4. AI-generated content answers

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **118** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] Grid letters are crisp and easy to loop; there's room to circle words without hitting neighbours.
- [ ] The clue letter boxes under each grid are big enough to write in.
- [ ] Case openers (pages 7, 16, 25, 34, 43, 52, 61, 70, 79, 88) show their illustration cleanly, and solid blacks print evenly.
- [ ] The Solutions opener (page 97) is a right-hand page; on solution pages the gray word loops and black leftover-letter dots are readable.
- [ ] Nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
