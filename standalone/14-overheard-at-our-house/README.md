**Printing cost source:** KDP Help, [Hardcover Printing Cost](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H) (fetched 2026-11-04). Black-ink hardcover on Amazon.com, regular trim: **75–108 pages is a flat $6.80**; 110–550 pages is $5.65 + $0.012 per page. Minimum list price = 6.80 ÷ 0.60 = **$11.33**. (108 pages is the top of the flat band, so adding even two pages would raise printing to $6.97.)

**Cover size formula:** KDP Help, [Create a Hardcover Cover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6), the same maths as *Twenty-Five Christmases* (12):

- wrap = **15 mm = 0.5906 in** on every outside edge
- board = trim + 5 mm wide, + 6 mm tall = **6.1969 × 9.2362 in**
- spine = pages × 0.002252 in + 4.8 mm (0.1890 in) = **0.4322 in** (white paper)
- hinge = **10 mm (0.3937 in)** each side of the spine, kept free of text. Text and art sit at least 0.63 in (16 mm) from the book edges.
- **width = 2 × 0.5906 + 2 × 6.1969 + 0.4322 = 14.0070 in**
- **height = 2 × 0.5906 + 9.2362 = 10.4173 in**
- **Check:** KDP's [cover calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/cover-templates) (Hardcover, 6 × 9, white paper, 108 pages) should give the same size.
- Barcode: a white 2 × 1.2 in box, 0.76 in above the bottom board edge and 0.25 in from the hinge. KDP prints the barcode there.

## 2. Create the HARDCOVER in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Hardcover**.
2. Fill in **Hardcover Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Hardcover Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**. Publication date → leave blank. Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `standalone-14-overheard-at-our-house-interior.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `standalone-14-overheard-at-our-house-cover.pdf`. This is the **hardcover** wrap, not a paperback cover.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Hardcover Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**. Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**. List price **$16.99** (60% royalty, about $3.39 a copy).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Hardcover Book**. Publishing by mid-November gives it a chance as a Christmas gift for parents and grandparents.
