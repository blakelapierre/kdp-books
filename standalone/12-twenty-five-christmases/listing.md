<b>One book. Twenty-five Christmases. A whole family's story.</b>

<i>Twenty-Five Christmases</i> is a keepsake hardcover for writing down your family's holidays as they happen, one Christmas at a time. Each year has its own <b>four illustrated pages</b>: a framed space for a photo or this year's Christmas card, who was there, how the tree looked, Christmas Eve and Christmas morning, what was on the table, the gifts to remember, the best moment, the biggest laugh and a wish for next year.
