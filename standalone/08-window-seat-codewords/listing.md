## What's inside
- 120 codewords on 13 × 13 British-style (lattice) grids, 28 to 31 answers each, every answer 3–9 letters
- 40 Easy (6 starter letters, only the most common words), 40 Medium (4 starters), 40 Hard (2 starters, a wider vocabulary)
- Every grid uses all 26 letters; no answer repeats inside a grid
- Answers come from the ENABLE word list, kept only if common in everyday English (wordfreq Zipf ≥ 3.3, ≥ 3.8 for Easy), with proper nouns (anything the American hunspell dictionary lists only capitalised), British spellings, abbreviations, rude words and violent or gloomy words filtered out
- Front matter: title, copyright, contents, frontispiece, How to Solve (with a worked KETTLE example), Solving Tips
- Each puzzle page: the numbered grid with starters filled in, a 1–26 key, an A–Z cross-off line, a small vignette
- Back matter: six solutions per page (finished grid plus the full key), thank-you page with "Also by Blake La Pierre"

## Estimated royalty per copy (Amazon.com)
KDP black-ink paperback, regular trim: $2.30 flat for 24–110 pages, otherwise **$1.00 + $0.012 per page**. This book is 154 pages.

- Printing cost = $1.00 + 154 × $0.012 = **$2.848 ≈ $2.85**
- Royalty = 60% × list price − printing cost
- At **$9.99**: 0.60 × 9.99 − 2.848 = 5.994 − 2.848 = **$3.15 per copy**
