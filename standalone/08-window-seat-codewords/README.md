## 2. Create the paperback in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Paperback**.
2. Fill in **Paperback Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Paperback Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**; Publication date → leave blank; Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `interior-standalone-08-window-seat-codewords.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `cover-standalone-08-window-seat-codewords.pdf`.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Paperback Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**; Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**; list price **$9.99** (60% royalty, about $3.15/copy). Expanded Distribution is optional (40% rate, about $1.15/copy).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **154** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] The tiny numbers in the grid squares (and in the key) are readable in print, and there's room left in each square to write a letter.
- [ ] Starter letters appear in every square with their number and in the key, and are struck through on the A–Z line.
- [ ] The frontispiece (page 4) and section openers print as crisp black ink: solid blacks are even, no gray haze.
- [ ] Section openers (pages 7, 49 and 91) and the Solutions opener (page 133) are right-hand pages.
- [ ] Nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
