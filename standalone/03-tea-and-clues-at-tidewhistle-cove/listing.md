## Description
```html
<b>Pour a cup of tea, pull up a chair by the window, and see if you can solve it before Agnes does.</b>

In the little seaside village of Tidewhistle Cove, a prize cake vanishes from the summer show, a garden gnome disappears overnight, and a parrot suddenly learns a brand new phrase. Agnes Bell, who runs the Kettle and Gull tea room, notices everything and knows how everyone takes their tea.

Thirty short, gentle mysteries, each one about five minutes long. Every story ends with <b>Can you solve it?</b>, a pause to think, and then the solution, explained step by step.

<b>Made for reading or listening:</b>
<ul>
<li>30 cozy mini-mysteries with one fair, clear answer each</li>
<li>The solution comes right after every case, so there's no flipping to the back</li>
<li>No grids, charts or pictures. Every clue is in the words, so it works read aloud, in the car or at bedtime</li>
<li>Clue stories plus seven little logic cases: who's telling the truth, who sat where, who could have got there in time</li>
<li>Clean and kind. No murders and nobody gets hurt, only missing treasures, small fibs and borrowed belongings</li>
</ul>

A lovely pick for fans of cozy mysteries, brain teasers and solve-it-yourself stories, and for families who like to guess together.

Agnes has kept a table for you by the window.
```
