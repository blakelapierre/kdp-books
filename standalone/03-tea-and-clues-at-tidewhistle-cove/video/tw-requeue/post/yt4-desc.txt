The Kettle and Gull has four little tables in a row, from the door (table 1) to the big bay window (table 4). 🪑

Kerensa Hale left her silver seagull brooch on the windowsill, and the sill can only be reached from the window table. Four guests had lunch, one at each table, and nobody got up until they left. By two o'clock, the brooch was gone.

Young Pip did the serving, and he remembers a few things:

Captain Quill sat right next to Wenna.
Loveday wasn't at either end of the row.
Mr Spargo sat closer to the door than Captain Quill.
Wenna wasn't at the window table: she complained she couldn't see the boats.

Agnes drew four little squares in her mind.
