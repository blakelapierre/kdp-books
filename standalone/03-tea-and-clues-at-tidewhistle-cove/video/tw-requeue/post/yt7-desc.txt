The village hall in Tidewhistle Cove has a spare key, hidden under the big terracotta plant pot by the front door. It hangs on a short blue ribbon so it's easy to find in the dark, and only the hall committee knows about it. 🔑

On Saturday night, the raffle hamper (jams, chutneys and a whole Cornish cheese) vanished from the locked hall. No broken window, no forced door: whoever took it let themselves in with a key and locked up again.

On Sunday, Constable Ollie lifted the pot, looked underneath and set it down. All he said was that the spare key was still there. Then he and Agnes spoke to three helpers:

Demelza Rowe left at five and went straight to her sister's for supper (her sister confirms it).
Pip Carew went home at six and had no idea there was a spare key at all.
Mr Spargo, who built the tombola stall: "A spare key? Under a plant pot? I had no idea. And I'm sure I'd remember a key on a blue ribbon like that."

Ollie and Agnes looked at each other.
