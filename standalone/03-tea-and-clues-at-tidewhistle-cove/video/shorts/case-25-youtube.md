For three mornings in a row, a silver teaspoon has gone missing from the Kettle and Gull. Every night Agnes Bell leaves a tray of clean spoons on the sideboard under the back window, locks both doors and keeps the only keys in her apron pocket. The back window stays open at the top, just a hand's width: nobody could squeeze through, not even a small child. 🥄

Monday: one spoon gone. Tuesday: another. Wednesday: a third, plus a scrap of shiny silver foil. Never touched: the lifeboat coin jar, the wooden spoons, a dull pewter spoon, the sugar bowl, a plate of shortbread.

Pip can't stand teaspoons. Mr Fenwick was at the grocer's from five, and his driver agrees. Loveday was home in bed with Pickle the terrier, and neither has keys.

On Wednesday Agnes finds something on the back windowsill, right under the gap: a long, glossy black feather with a patch of bright white near the end.
