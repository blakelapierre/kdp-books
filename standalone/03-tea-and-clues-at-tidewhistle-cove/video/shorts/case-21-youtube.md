The little museum above the post office has a glass case of old coins washed up on Tidewhistle Cove beach. The best of them are silver Spanish coins, worn smooth by the sea, with a crown stamped on one side. On Wednesday night someone lifted the lid and took a velvet bag holding six of them. 🪙

On Thursday Jenna Tonkin opened her ice cream van at ten. Every evening she empties her till completely; every morning she fills it with a sealed bag of new coins from the bank. At eleven she hurried into the Kettle and Gull with a Spanish coin on her palm: it was right on top of the coins in her till. Only three customers all morning, because of the sea mist:

Demelza Rowe, lemon sorbet: tapped her card.
