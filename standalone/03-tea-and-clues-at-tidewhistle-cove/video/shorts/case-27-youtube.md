At the end of the Tidewhistle harbour wall stands the foghorn. Whenever fog rolls in over the bay it sounds all by itself, a deep boom every half a minute until the fog clears, and you can hear it from every house in the village. 🌫️

On Tuesday night Agnes Bell has a cold and can't sleep. She sits up in bed with her window open from ten o'clock until three in the morning. The night is clear and starry, and the foghorn never sounds once.

On Wednesday morning the yacht moored in the bay is missing its brass barometer. Someone rowed out in a dinghy during the night.

Captain Quill was on night watch at the harbour office with two lifeboat crew. Tamsin was at her sister's in town and caught the first bus back. Mr Keast, a visitor who'd admired the yacht's brass all week: "I was at home all night. The fog was so thick you couldn't see your hand in front of your face.
