The old lighthouse on the point is a little museum now, and Hedley Truscott is its keeper. In the lamp room stands a polished brass telescope that belonged to the very first keeper. ⏱️

On Tuesday at two o'clock exactly, Hedley climbed out onto the gallery to clean the windows. At a quarter past two he came back in. In those fifteen minutes, the telescope disappeared.

Three people were each seen twice that afternoon:

Wenna Polglaze: in the Kettle and Gull at 2:00, and again at 2:20.
Mr Opie, a visitor with binoculars round his neck: at the harbour at 1:45, and again at 2:30.
Pip Carew: in the bookshop at 1:55, and again at 2:10.

The paths up to the point are steep and rocky, and nobody can do them any faster: Kettle and Gull to the lighthouse is 15 minutes, harbour 20 minutes, bookshop 10 minutes, and it takes just as long coming back down.

Agnes decided to do it in her head.
