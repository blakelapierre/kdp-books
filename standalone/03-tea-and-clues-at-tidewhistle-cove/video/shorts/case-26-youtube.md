At the Tidewhistle summer fair, the raffle prize is a hamper so big it takes two people to lift. Loveday Nance buys a single ticket, tucks it into the top of her basket and joins the queue at the tea stall. 🎟️

Five people stand in that queue, one behind the other. A gust lifts the ticket out of Loveday's basket, and it's picked up by the person standing DIRECTLY behind her. At three o'clock the vicar draws Loveday's number, but somebody else hands it in and claims the hamper.

The five in the queue: Mr Fenwick, Loveday, Pip Carew, Demelza Rowe and Hedley Truscott. Agnes, serving the tea, remembers:
Mr Fenwick was at the very front.
Hedley was somewhere behind Pip.
Loveday was exactly in the middle.
Demelza wasn't last, and wasn't right next to Mr Fenwick.
