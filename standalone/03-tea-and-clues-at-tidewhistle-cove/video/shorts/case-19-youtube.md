Above the door of the sailing club hangs a brass ship's bell, rung for the start of every race. Its hook is so high that even the tallest person in the village has to stretch up on tiptoe to reach it. The tallest person in the village is Tobias Ferris of the lifeboat crew: six and a half feet tall. 🔔

The club's stepladder lives in a locked cupboard, and Agnes, the club treasurer, keeps the only key on her own key ring. On Sunday the cupboard was still locked with the ladder inside, and the only other thing in the porch to stand on was a tiny stool that has been broken for months. And the bell was gone from its hook.

Three people were at the club's quiz night on Saturday:

Loveday Nance, a little over five feet tall: at the quiz table all evening; she couldn't reach that hook standing on a chair.
Mr Fenwick, not much taller than Loveday: left early with a headache.
Tobias: at the quiz too, home at ten. "I didn't touch the bell. I hardly ever even notice it."

Agnes jingled her key ring and looked up at the empty hook.
