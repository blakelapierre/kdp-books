## 2. Create the paperback in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Paperback**.
2. Fill in **Paperback Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Paperback Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**; Publication date → leave blank; Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Glossy**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `standalone-01-the-advent-clock-interior.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `standalone-01-the-advent-clock-cover.pdf`.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Paperback Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**; Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**; list price **$9.99** (60% royalty, about $3.69/copy). Expanded Distribution is optional (40% rate).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

## 4. AI-generated content answers

KDP asks this on the Content page. Truthful answers for this book:

- **Texts: Yes.** The text (how-to pages, stories/frames, descriptions) was written with AI. Choose *Entire work* and the editing level that matches how much you changed it.
- **Images: Yes.** The cover and interior illustrations were drawn by AI-written code. Choose *Entire work* and the editing level that fits.

## 5. Proof check (Previewer or printed proof)

- [ ] Page count in the KDP previewer matches **92** and the spine text sits centred on the spine with no wrap-around onto the covers.
- [ ] The frontispiece (page 2) and title-page art print as crisp black ink: solid blacks are even, no gray haze, no banding.
- [ ] Small vignettes never touch a puzzle, a grid, a clue or the page number, and nothing sits closer than 0.375 in to the outside edge.
- [ ] Blank pages show only the small centred vignette; section openers start on right-hand pages.
- [ ] Author on the front cover, spine and title page reads **Blake La Pierre** only.
- [ ] The back-cover barcode box is empty (KDP prints the barcode there).
- [ ] The 15×15 nonogram clues (doors 6 and 21), the Morse table (door 16) and the logic grid (door 9) are readable.
- [ ] No illustration before the Midnight page gives away an answer (no candle, bell, star or clock tower drawn early).
- [ ] Spine text (7.5 pt on a 0.207 in spine) is centred and legible.
