```html
<b>One cup for each of you. One question at a time.</b>

<i>Two Cups of Tea</i> is a cozy, year-long question journal for couples. Each week brings one gentle theme and three thoughtful questions, and every question has <b>separate answer space for each of you</b>, so you can write on your own, swap, and discover something new about the person across the table.

Choose who is the <b>Teacup</b> and who is the <b>Mug</b>, put the kettle on, and begin wherever you are: dating, engaged, newlyweds, or many happy years in. The questions are warm and wholesome, about first impressions, childhood summers, favourite meals, small kindnesses, family traditions and the dreams you share.

<b>Inside you'll find:</b>
<ul>
<li>52 weekly themes and 156 questions, arranged through spring, summer, autumn and winter</li>
<li>Two labelled answer spaces on every question, one for each partner</li>
<li>A small "together this week" idea to tick off, from watching a sunset to cooking a family recipe</li>
<li>A "this week I appreciated" line for each of you</li>
<li>Season check-ins with a framed page for a photo, ticket stub or sketch</li>
<li>Our Story So Far, Where We Are Today and Our Someday List pages</li>
<li>A Year in Review and a letter from each of you to the other</li>
<li>Charming black-and-white illustrations: a garden bench in spring, a seaside picnic, a porch in autumn and a fireside winter evening</li>
<li>A 6 × 9 inch case-laminate hardcover made to keep</li>
</ul>

A thoughtful gift for anniversaries, weddings, engagements, Valentine's Day and Christmas, and a lovely way to slow down together, one cup of tea at a time.
```
