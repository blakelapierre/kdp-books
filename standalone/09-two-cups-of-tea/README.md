**Printing cost source:** KDP Help, [Hardcover Printing Cost](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHT976ZKSKUXBB6H) (fetched 2026-10-21). Black-ink hardcover with 110–550 pages on Amazon.com, regular trim: **$5.65 per book + $0.012 per page**. KDP's own example: 300 pages = $9.25. Minimum list price = printing cost ÷ 0.60 = **$12.18**.

**Cover size formula:** KDP Help, [Create a Hardcover Cover](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDTKFJPNQCBTMRV6) (fetched 2026-10-21), plus the same wrap maths as our earlier hardcovers (*The Advent Clock*, *Stories We Keep*, *Fences of Frostwood*):

- wrap = **15 mm = 0.5906 in** on every outside edge. KDP's page prints "0.51” (15 mm)", but 15 mm is 0.591 in, and 0.591 is the value our earlier hardcovers used.
- board = trim + 5 mm wide, + 6 mm tall = **6.1969 × 9.2362 in**
- spine = pages × 0.002252 in + 4.8 mm (0.1890 in) = **0.4998 in** (white paper)
- hinge = **10 mm (0.3937 in)** each side of the spine, kept free of text. Text and art sit at least **0.635 in (16 mm)** from the book edges.
- **width = 2 × 0.5906 + 2 × 6.1969 + 0.4998 = 14.0746 in**
- **height = 2 × 0.5906 + 9.2362 = 10.4173 in**
- A third-party calculator ([paperbackmath.com](https://paperbackmath.com/kdp-hardcover-cover-calculator), which says it was checked against KDP's specs in September 2026) uses the same constants. It gives a height of 10.417 in for 6 × 9, which matches.
- **Check:** KDP's [cover calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/cover-templates) (Hardcover, 6 × 9, white paper, 138 pages) should give the same size. Download its template if you want to compare.
- Barcode: a white 2 × 1.2 in box, 0.76 in above the bottom board edge and 0.25 in from the hinge. KDP prints the barcode there.

## 2. Create the HARDCOVER in KDP

1. **kdp.amazon.com** → **Bookshelf** → **+ Create** → **Hardcover**.
2. Fill in **Hardcover Details** with the copy-paste blocks in section 3.
3. **Hardcover Content**: ISBN → **Get a free KDP ISBN**. Publication date → leave blank. Print options → **Black & white interior with white paper**, trim **6 × 9 in**, **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**.
4. **Manuscript** → upload `standalone-09-two-cups-of-tea-interior.pdf`.
5. **Book cover** → **Upload a cover you already have** → upload `standalone-09-two-cups-of-tea-cover.pdf`. This is the **hardcover** wrap, not a paperback cover.
6. Answer the **AI-generated content** questions (section 4).
7. **Launch Previewer** → check every item in section 5 → **Approve** → **Save and Continue**.
8. **Hardcover Rights & Pricing**: Territories **All territories**. Primary marketplace **Amazon.com**. List price **$19.99** (60% royalty, about $4.69 a copy).
9. Order a **proof copy** first if you can (section 5), then **Publish Your Hardcover Book**.
